## Product decisions (confirmed by Henson, 2026-10-09)

| ID   | Decision                               | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| P-01 | Who collects payments                  | Stripe Connect only. Shop is merchant of record via **direct charges** on its connected account; platform takes an `application_fee_amount`. Platform never holds client or seller funds. If Stripe Connect is not available for a UAE platform with UAE shops, online checkout does not ship and shops stay contact-only plus cash on delivery.                                            |
| P-02 | Seller verification                    | Basic KYC (who the seller is) for every seller. Enabling checkout additionally requires a trade licence validated by an admin.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| P-03 | Mobile                                 | Installable web app (PWA) first; native apps only later if usage proves the need.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| P-04 | Launch area                            | All seven emirates. Sellers are already being onboarded in each. Search opens in an emirate once it has verified shops.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| P-05 | Business model                         | Free listings plus a commission on checkout orders.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| P-06 | Delivery                               | Sellers deliver themselves. A delivery product and third-party couriers are a future product line.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| P-07 | Commission on cash on delivery         | COD orders are commission-free at launch; revisit once checkout volume is known.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| P-08 | Paid features for contact-only sellers | Boosts (sponsored placement) launch in Phase 2. Pro plan in Phase 4. No pay-per-lead. Checkout-only perks (verified-purchase reviews, ranked "Order online" button) from Phase 3.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| P-09 | Sign-in codes                          | Confirmed 2026-10-11. One-time codes are sent on **WhatsApp** by default. SMS is an optional fallback and may be removed because of its cost. This needs a WhatsApp Business account with an approved authentication template. The wait before a resend starts at 30 seconds and doubles with each code sent in the hour (30 s, 1 min, 2 min, 4 min), on top of 5 codes per phone per hour. |
| P-10 | Social sign-in                         | Confirmed 2026-10-11. Phase 1 offers **Google and Facebook** sign-in. Apple sign-in is dropped from the plan. Until Phase 1 the sign-in screen shows phone and email only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

Business prerequisites before Phase 3 (not engineering tasks, but they gate it): the company's UAE
trade licence with an e-commerce activity, a corporate bank account, and Stripe's written confirmation
that Connect supports a UAE platform with UAE connected accounts, direct charges and application fees.

| T-32 | 2026-10-17 | Nearby search ranking and speed (F1-06).
